Privacy Policy

App: Hourly Chime & Water Reminder (formerly “Blip: Daily Reminders”; Android package com.rork.blip)
Developer / legal entity: HamsterDragon Studios
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HamsterDragon Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in the Android app Hourly Chime & Water Reminder, previously published as “Blip: Daily Reminders” (the “App”), which is available on Google Play. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

  • The App has no sign-up or account system. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • Your timer, reminder, sound, and theme settings are stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information (see Section 3).
  • Optional subscriptions (“Blip Pro”, the App’s premium plan) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment card details.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following only in local storage on your device, so that it works as you configured it. This data is not sent to us or to any server operated by us:

  • Interval timer settings (minutes or hours, start mode)
  • Reminder times and intervals (for example water, medicine, meals, eye rest, posture)
  • Sound, vibration, volume, theme, and display preferences
  • Your saved timer profiles
  • Whether you currently have an active Blip Pro subscription

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays banner ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID, where available and permitted by your device settings)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and software information (such as device model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (such as ad impressions and clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), consent choices for personalized ads may be presented to you. Subscribing to Blip Pro removes ads from the App.

4. Purchases and subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Blip Pro is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play Billing. Payments, billing information, and subscription management are handled by Google. We receive only the information needed to confirm that your subscription is active (such as purchase status and product identifier). We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

6. How we use and share information

We do not sell your personal information. The only parties that receive information through the App are Google (for advertising through AdMob and for payments through Google Play), as described above. We do not share data stored locally on your device with anyone.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the data described in Section 2 lives only on your device, you control it: it is kept until you change it, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google through AdMob or Google Play is retained and can be managed according to Google’s policies and your Google Account settings. If you would like us to address any question about data related to the App, contact us using the details in Section 10.

8. Security

We keep the App simple on purpose: we operate no user accounts and do not transmit your settings to our servers, which reduces the data that could be exposed. Communication with Google services such as AdMob and Google Play uses industry-standard encryption provided by those services.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will help address it.
