# Privacy Policy for Blip — Interval Timer & Daily Reminders

**Last updated: May 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Blip ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Blip is a simple interval timer and reminder app. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

- Your name, email address, or any personal identity information
- Your location or GPS data
- Your contacts, photos, or media files
- Your device's unique identifiers for tracking purposes
- Any usage analytics or behavioral data sent to our servers
- Any financial or payment information

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Blip stores the following data **only on your device** using local storage. This data never leaves your phone and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

- Your selected interval timer settings (minutes or hours)
- Your chosen start mode (From Now or Round Clock)
- Your sound and vibration preferences
- Your selected beep sound and theme
- Your enabled reminders (water, medicine, meals, eye rest, posture)
- Your reminder times and intervals
- Your app settings (24hr clock, keep screen on, etc.)

All of this data is stored locally and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

---

## 3. Permissions We Request and Why

Blip requests the following device permissions:

| Permission | Why We Need It |
|---|---|
| **Post Notifications** | To send reminder and interval beep notifications when the app is in the background |
| **Foreground Service** | To keep the interval timer running reliably in the background with minimum battery usage |
| **Vibrate** | To vibrate the phone at beep intervals when vibration is enabled by the user |
| **Receive Boot Completed** | To restore active reminders after the phone is restarted |

We do **not** request permissions for camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage.

---

## 4. Advertising

Blip's free version displays banner advertisements served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect certain non-personal device information to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy, which you can review at:

👉 https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device settings:
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → Turn off Personalized Ads

---

## 5. Third Party Services

Blip does not use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, social login, or tracking SDKs other than Google AdMob for advertising in the free version.

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your data with any third parties for any purpose.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Blip is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take immediate steps to delete such information.

---

## 7. Data Security

Since Blip does not collect or transmit any personal data to external servers, there is no risk of your personal data being accessed, leaked, or breached through our systems. All your preferences and settings remain solely on your device.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

---

## 9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete on our end. All data stored locally on your device can be cleared by uninstalling the app.
